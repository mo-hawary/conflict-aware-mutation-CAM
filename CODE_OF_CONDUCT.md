# Code of Conduct

CAM expects professional, respectful collaboration.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss code, behavior, evidence, and trade-offs rather than attacking people.
- Give technically specific feedback and assume good faith until evidence suggests otherwise.
- Respect different backgrounds, experience levels, and communication styles.
- Accept correction and keep disagreements focused on the work.
- Do not harass, threaten, discriminate against, dox, or deliberately disrupt contributors.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove abusive content, close interactions, or restrict participation when behavior makes productive collaboration unsafe or impractical.

For private conduct reports, email **dev.elhawary@gmail.com** with the subject **CAM CONDUCT**. Reports will be handled as confidentially as reasonably possible.
